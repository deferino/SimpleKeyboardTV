# Privacy

Simple Keyboard TV does not collect, store or send your data. Anything you type stays on your device.

- The app requests no permissions at all, not even Internet, so it cannot send anything over the network.
- Your preferences (language, theme, size) are stored on the device. If you have Android backup turned on,
  the system may include those preferences in its own backup.

The app has no ads, no analytics and no accounts.
